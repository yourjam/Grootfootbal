📊 Rank is now driven by real value-over-replacement (VBD), not ADP. For offense/K/DEF, I pulled FantasyPros' consensus season-stat projections (raw yards/TDs/etc., not generic rankings) and ran them through your league's exact scoring rules — Half PPR, Pass TD=4, Rush/Rec TD=6, and so on — to get each player's real projected points. "Rank" and "Tier" are now sorted by value-over-replacement (points above the last startable player at that position), so tier cliffs reflect actual point gaps instead of an approximated ADP curve. The Value column shows each player's VBD (higher = bigger edge over your bench-level alternative at that position). Two honest gaps: FantasyPros' free tier only publishes the top 10 QBs/TEs/DSTs with full raw stats — players past that were extrapolated with a decay curve rather than individually projected, so treat QB/DST value past the top 10 as lower-confidence. Kicker scoring is a blended estimate (FG make total × ~3.6, no distance breakdown) — low precision, but this barely matters since kickers are widely known to be close to unpredictable noise year over year anyway.
📈 Context Score (was "Boom Factor") — recalibrated. This replaced the old binary BOOM/no-BOOM flag with a graded 0–100 score so you can see magnitude, not just a yes/no. Two upgrades from before: (1) schedule strength now uses your team's full 2026 season average, blended 55/45 with a dedicated fantasy-playoff-window average (weeks 15–17, League Station) weighted toward the playoffs since that's what actually decides your championship — not just the first 5 weeks. (2) O-line sensitivity is now position-specific instead of one flat rule: RB is weighted most on O-line quality (0.55), QB moderately (0.35), WR lightly (0.20) since receiver production leans more on scheme/target share than blocking, and TE gets a mild inverse weight (checkdown/safety-valve theory — a worse O-line can modestly help a receiving TE's floor). RB is now included at all, which the old rule skipped entirely. Score bands: 72+ BOOM, 58–71 lean up, 42–57 neutral, 28–41 lean down, <28 FADE. The 0–100 score itself is still O-line + schedule context only — it does not blend in usage. This will be replaced/supplemented once you upload the official 2026 Boom Factor sheet. 🛡 IDP (D) is new — read before trusting it blindly. Your league scores 3 individual defensive players (any of LB/DL/DB) plus your DEF unit. Your scoring gives solo tackles 1pt, sacks only 1pt, and zero points for passes defended — that combination heavily favors tackling linebackers, then box safeties/DBs, with pass-rush DL scoring lowest despite name recognition. IDP rank/timing here is a build from Fantasy Six Pack's Aug 1, 2026 positional rankings blended with that scoring-based value order, not a mainstream ADP feed (IDP ADP isn't well-tracked industry-wide) — treat the exact overall placement as directional, but the LB > DB > DL priority order is scoring-driven and should hold. Note: offense/K/DEF now run on real projected-points VBD (see below), but IDP does not — I don't have access to bulk per-player defensive stat projections, so rather than invent point totals and present them with false precision next to real data, IDP stays on this rank-based proxy. It's spliced into the board at a value-equivalent slot next to its real-data neighbors, so the overall ordering should still feel right — just know the offense numbers are measured and the IDP numbers are estimated.

Draft day workflow

On draft day, open your Yahoo draft in Chrome — Claude reads the live board and calls out picks as they happen. Use this board as your visual backup: mark a player Mine when you draft them, or Off board when another team takes them, and the recommendation updates instantly.

⭐ / 🚫 flags: tap the star or stop-sign next to any player (table row or a rec card) to mark your own take — favorite gives a small score bump, bust gives a bigger penalty so they drop down the list without being blocked outright. Tap the same icon again to clear it. Flags survive a draft reset.

Bye-week collision: the engine now docks a player's score if their bye overlaps another player you've already drafted at a position that competes for the same lineup slot (QB, the RB/WR/TE/FLEX group, or your 3 IDP slots) — so it won't walk you into stacking three same-bye RBs without at least flagging it in the reason line.
