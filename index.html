Methodology & data notes — how rank, value, and Context Score are actually built (worth a read once)
📊 Rank is now driven by real value-over-replacement (VBD), not ADP. For offense/K/DEF, I pulled FantasyPros' consensus season-stat projections (raw yards/TDs/etc., not generic rankings) and ran them through your league's exact scoring rules — Half PPR, Pass TD=4, Rush/Rec TD=6, and so on — to get each player's real projected points. "Rank" and "Tier" are now sorted by value-over-replacement (points above the last startable player at that position), so tier cliffs reflect actual point gaps instead of an approximated ADP curve. The Value column shows each player's VBD (higher = bigger edge over your bench-level alternative at that position). Two honest gaps: FantasyPros' free tier only publishes the top 10 QBs/TEs/DSTs with full raw stats — players past that were extrapolated with a decay curve rather than individually projected, so treat QB/DST value past the top 10 as lower-confidence. Kicker scoring is a blended estimate (FG make total × ~3.6, no distance breakdown) — low precision, but this barely matters since kickers are widely known to be close to unpredictable noise year over year anyway.
📈 Context Score (was "Boom Factor") — recalibrated, then bug-fixed. This replaced the old binary BOOM/no-BOOM flag with a graded 0–100 score so you can see magnitude, not just a yes/no. Schedule strength blends 55% regular-season difficulty with 45% fantasy-playoff-window difficulty (weeks 15–17), weighted toward the playoffs since that's what actually decides your championship. O-line sensitivity is position-specific: RB weighted most (0.55), QB moderate (0.35), WR light (0.20) since receiver production leans more on scheme/target share than blocking, and TE gets a mild inverse weight (checkdown/safety-valve theory). Fix (found via the original cheat sheet creator's own Reddit writeup, which explicitly splits weeks 1–14 vs. 15–17): the schedule blend originally used a 17-week season average for the "regular season" half, which silently double-counted weeks 15–17 in both halves of the blend. Weeks 1–14 are now backed out algebraically so the two halves are genuinely non-overlapping — this moved 99 of 125 offense scores and flipped the band label (e.g. BOOM → lean up) for 22 players, mostly PHI/DEN/SF/TEN/CLE skill players. Score bands: 72+ BOOM, 58–71 lean up, 42–57 neutral, 28–41 lean down, <28 FADE. The 0–100 score itself is still O-line + schedule context only — it does not blend in usage. This will be replaced/supplemented once you upload the official 2026 Boom Factor sheet. Rank/tier/position are live 2026 ADP-adjacent VBD data, current as of the date above.
📶 '25 usage data added (shown in notes, not folded into Context Score). Pulled real 2025 regular-season target share and snap counts from FantasySP for WR/TE/RB (90 of 109 skill players matched — the 19 unmatched, e.g. Garrett Wilson, Malik Nabers, Sam LaPorta, fell outside the top-100 WR/TE or top-91 RB target/touch leaderboard, generally due to injury-shortened 2025 seasons; no usage line shown for them rather than guessing). Target share is targets ÷ each team's total tracked targets (WR+TE+RB, so it's a true share, not a raw count). Red zone target/carry share was not included — every source I checked (FantasySP, DraftSharks) paywalls it past the top ~10-25 players, and I'd rather leave it out than half-fill it with guesses. If you want, I can pull real red-zone data next from nflverse's raw play-by-play instead of a paywalled leaderboard — it'd take more work but it's the actual gold-standard source and fully open.
🛡 IDP (D) is new — read before trusting it blindly. Your league scores 3 individual defensive players (any of LB/DL/DB) plus your DEF unit. Your scoring gives solo tackles 1pt, sacks only 1pt, and zero points for passes defended — that combination heavily favors tackling linebackers, then box safeties/DBs, with pass-rush DL scoring lowest despite name recognition. IDP rank/timing here is a build from Fantasy Six Pack's Aug 1, 2026 positional rankings blended with that scoring-based value order, not a mainstream ADP feed (IDP ADP isn't well-tracked industry-wide) — treat the exact overall placement as directional, but the LB > DB > DL priority order is scoring-driven and should hold. Note: offense/K/DEF now run on real projected-points VBD (see below), but IDP does not — I don't have access to bulk per-player defensive stat projections, so rather than invent point totals and present them with false precision next to real data, IDP stays on this rank-based proxy. It's spliced into the board at a value-equivalent slot next to its real-data neighbors, so the overall ordering should still feel right — just know the offense numbers are measured and the IDP numbers are estimated.
🧮 New engine additions (Aug 2, 2026) — what's real vs. approximated. Dynamic VORP: value is now recomputed live off currently-available supply at each position (not a static pre-draft baseline) — when a position's remaining pool gets too thin to give a meaningful live estimate, it falls back to the pre-draft baseline rather than degrading into "whoever's worst is left." Pick Predictor (Survive%): grounded in real ADP + standard deviation from FantasyFootballCalculator's 2026 PPR report (4,121 real mock drafts, pulled Aug 1, 2026) — modeled as a normal distribution over draft position. It does not yet condition on which specific players your opponents have taken beyond removing drafted players from the pool; full opponent-specific modeling (Rival Profiling) is a planned follow-up once live sync is validated. Players with no FFC match (all IDP, and players outside FFC's ~top 250) fall back to a rank-derived variance estimate, marked with a "~" in the UI. Custom rule adapters: Superflex and Keeper cost are fully wired into scoring math. TE Premium is estimated from each player's real 2025 targets (not a final 2026 reception projection) — treat it as directional, not precise. Points Per First Down is listed in Custom Rules but intentionally left uncomputed: no free bulk first-down projection source was found, so it's flagged rather than faked. Live sync: a separate Tampermonkey script (fantasy-draft-sync.user.js) reads picks off your authenticated Yahoo draft tab and mirrors them here — no OAuth, no API keys, nothing that touches your Yahoo login. It could not be tested against a live/mock Yahoo draft room during development (no active draft to observe), so it ships with three parallel detection strategies plus a manual fallback panel — run a Yahoo mock draft with it installed before Aug 29 to confirm it's catching picks.

Draft day workflow

On draft day, open your Yahoo draft in Chrome — Claude reads the live board and calls out picks as they happen. Use this board as your visual backup: mark a player Mine when you draft them, or Off board when another team takes them, and the recommendation updates instantly.

⭐ / 🚫 flags: tap the star or stop-sign next to any player (table row or a rec card) to mark your own take — favorite gives a small score bump, bust gives a bigger penalty so they drop down the list without being blocked outright. Tap the same icon again to clear it. Flags survive a draft reset.

Bye-week collision: the engine now docks a player's score if their bye overlaps another player you've already drafted at a position that competes for the same lineup slot (QB, the RB/WR/TE/FLEX group, or your 3 IDP slots) — so it won't walk you into stacking three same-bye RBs without at least flagging it in the reason line.
